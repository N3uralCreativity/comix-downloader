Privacy Policy and Legal Notice

Last updated: 24 September 2026 - Effective: 24 September 2026

This policy covers the free Comix Downloader extension and the optional Comix Downloader Plus service: the account, the Cloud Library, the Release Agenda, encrypted sync, and the web library. The free extension works without an account, and nothing is sent to Plus unless you sign in.

Free mode: no account is required, no reading analytics are sent to the Developer, and every free feature stays free. Settings, logs, reading data, downloads, and credentials stay in your browser, except for requests you direct to comix.to or comix.ws, your own library server, or the anonymous community service.

Plus: an account you create with your email. Cloud Library pages and names, synced settings and reading data, and restore points are encrypted on your device before upload, so the service stores them without being able to read them. The service does see your email, your subscription and devices, and how much storage you use.

01 Scope and controller

Comix Downloader is a browser extension published by N3uralCreativity (the Developer, we, or us). Comix Downloader Plus is an optional paid service for that extension, with an account, a Cloud Library, a Release Agenda, encrypted sync, a web portal, and a web library. The Developer is the controller of the personal data described here. This policy does not cover comix.to, comix.ws, image delivery networks, a library server you configure, browser stores, Stripe, Link, Resend, or Cloudflare, which process data under their own policies.

Comix Downloader and Plus are independent projects and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by comix.to or comix.ws.

02 Free extension data

Stored locally

The free extension can store settings, per-series presets, download markers, watched series, resume positions, reading statistics, followed-series chapter-age observations and inferred agenda state (used by the Plus Release Agenda), technical logs, running-download checkpoints, notice state, and review-prompt state in browser local storage. Push-to-library credentials also remain local and are excluded from settings exports.

Processed for requested functions

When you request a download, the extension reads public title and chapter information, opens chapter pages, fetches images, builds archives, and hands files to the browser download manager. When enabled, it can contact comix.to or comix.ws to check watched series, or contact a library endpoint you configured to upload a finished CBZ.

What free mode does not do

  • No account or Plus API request is made unless you explicitly create an account, sign in, or start Plus setup. When you are signed out, the account icon in the popup only opens the sign-in screen.
  • No advertising, behavioral analytics, fingerprinting, or sale of data is used.
  • Local logs, cookies, CAPTCHA information, library credentials, and Agenda history are never sent to Plus. Downloaded pages reach Plus only when you choose to save a chapter to the Cloud Library (Section 5).

03 Anonymous community service

The profile tenure badge, community chapter flags, and remote notices use a separate open-source Cloudflare Worker. It receives one-way hashes derived from public Comix identifiers, coarse flag counters, and the extension version needed to select a notice. It does not receive an email, readable user ID, title name, reading record, or downloaded content.

Cloudflare necessarily handles network information such as an IP address while delivering a request. The application does not intentionally store those addresses for community features.

04 Plus account and sign-in

Plus stays off until you sign in, from the extension popup, the Plus settings page, or the web portal. The first sign-in in the extension asks your browser for permission to contact the Plus service (and, in Firefox, for the optional data consent in Section 15). Plus then processes:

Email address
Used for six-digit sign-in codes, account identification, a reminder before a trial ends, payment problems, security notices such as a new-device sign-in, and deletion warnings. It is not added to a marketing list.
Authentication records
Hashed sign-in codes, hashed session tokens, expiry times, and revocation state. Raw tokens are stored by the extension or web library, not in readable form in the service database.
Device records
A random device ID, a device name such as browser and platform, a public ECDH key, approval state, and last-seen time. Up to five extension devices can be active; web sessions do not count toward that limit. A new device can read encrypted data only after an existing device approves it or you enter your recovery code, and we email you when a new device signs in.
Entitlement records
Trial, active, grace, cancellation, expiry, and renewal dates, plus Stripe customer and subscription identifiers. We do not receive full payment card details.
Storage records
For the Cloud Library: item counts, stored bytes, page counts and sizes, which item sits in which folder, upload and trash state, revision numbers, and timestamps. These enforce storage limits and keep devices in step.
Operational records
Request IDs, error codes, API timing, ciphertext size, schema version, revision number, and timestamps used for security and reliability. Logs must not include email, tokens, title names, reading records, file names, or ciphertext.
Rate-limit records
A keyed hash of the requesting IP address and time-window counters, used to limit sign-in code requests and API abuse. Counters are deleted after two days and are not used for tracking or advertising.

05 Cloud Library

The Cloud Library stores chapters you choose to save, so you can read them on your other devices and in the web library. Nothing is uploaded automatically, and items are private to your account: there is no sharing or publishing feature.

What is encrypted

Before upload, the extension encrypts every page with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your account encryption key, and binds each page to your account and to that item so it cannot be swapped with another. Chapter, folder, and series names, page file names, a small preview image of the first page, and your reading position in each chapter are encrypted the same way. Encrypted pages are stored in Cloudflare R2 and encrypted metadata in Cloudflare D1.

What the service can see

Your account, whether each item is a chapter or a folder and which folder it sits in, the number and encrypted size of its pages, a SHA-256 digest of each encrypted page used to verify uploads, upload and trash state, and timestamps. To avoid saving the same chapter twice, the extension also sends an identifier computed from the chapter address with your encryption key; the service cannot turn it back into a title or address.

Limits

Each account has a storage limit and an item limit, shown in the Cloud Library. Each encrypted page can be up to 8 MiB, and each chapter up to 1,000 pages.

On your devices

While a chapter uploads, its encrypted pages are kept in the browser's IndexedDB so an interrupted upload can resume. Your item list is cached, still encrypted, so the library opens offline. Pages you save for offline reading stay encrypted on the device until you open them, and you can remove them from a device at any time.

06 Release Agenda

The Release Agenda shows when chapters of the series you watch are expected. The extension builds it in your browser: it reads recent chapter ages from the title pages of series you follow on comix.to or comix.ws, combines them with the dates it first saw new chapters, and shows the result as a page on the Comix site. Release history and estimates are stored in the extension's local storage, are not a sync category, and are never sent to Plus. Plus is used only to confirm that your subscription is active. The watched series themselves sync only if you select the Watched series category.

07 End-to-end encrypted synchronization

You choose each category, and all of them start unchecked. The categories are settings and presets, watched series, reading progress and resume positions, downloaded-chapter markers, and reading statistics.

The extension serializes the selected data and encrypts it locally with AES-256-GCM. The service receives only the encrypted envelope and limited metadata: opaque account and device identifiers, revision, schema version, size, and timestamps. It stores ciphertext in Cloudflare R2 and metadata in D1. The service never receives the unwrapped encryption key.

The recovery code wraps the encryption key. Existing devices transfer that key to a new device using ECDH. Losing all approved devices and the recovery code makes cloud data unreadable; the only recovery is to verify the account email again and reset cloud encryption. That reset never deletes local extension data.

Sync never includes activity logs, active downloads, downloaded ZIP or CBZ files, library credentials, authentication cookies, notices, review state, CAPTCHA information, or Release Agenda history. Chapter pages go to Plus only through the Cloud Library.

08 Web portal and web library

The Plus web portal handles sign-in, billing, and account settings. The web library lets you read your Cloud Library in any browser and can be installed as an app.

Cookie
The portal sets one strictly necessary cookie, cdl_plus_session, which holds your sign-in session. It is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax, lasts up to 30 days, and is cleared when you sign out. Plus uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
Browser storage
The web library keeps its sign-in session, device keys, and theme choice in the browser's local storage. It unlocks encrypted items the same way as any new device: with approval from an existing device or your recovery code.
Offline use
A service worker caches the web library's own files so it opens without a connection. Pages you save offline stay encrypted in IndexedDB, as in Section 5.

Stripe and Link handle payment methods, applicable indirect taxes, invoices, renewals, refunds, transaction support, and legally required records. Plus receives only the identifiers and subscription state needed to grant access.

Stripe and Link deliver receipts and subscription messages. Resend delivers sign-in codes, new-device security alerts, the one-time seven-day trial reminder, and deletion warnings from plus@n3uralcreativity.top. Plus sends no newsletters, abandoned-cart campaigns, or promotional email.

A one-way email hash and Stripe customer ID may remain after account deletion solely to enforce one trial per verified account or customer and prevent repeated-trial abuse. This record is not used to contact, profile, or advertise to you.

11 Retention and deletion

  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes, allow five attempts, and are deleted within a day of expiring.
  • Access tokens expire after 15 minutes; rotating refresh sessions and the portal cookie expire after 30 days or on sign-out or revocation.
  • The latest encrypted sync state and one daily restore point are kept for 30 days. A limited number of Before restore points are also kept within that period.
  • Cloud Library items in the trash can be restored for 7 days, then they and their encrypted pages are permanently deleted. Deleting an item permanently from the trash removes it right away. Uploads left unfinished for 24 hours are removed.
  • Cancellation keeps Plus active through the paid or trial period. Failed payments receive a seven-day grace period.
  • Expired accounts are read-only for 90 days: you can still read, export, and delete your sync data and Cloud Library items, but not add new ones. After that, encrypted snapshots, sync metadata, and every Cloud Library item are deleted. We email a warning 7 days before.
  • Delete cloud data removes sync snapshots and leaves the account and Cloud Library in place. Reset cloud encryption removes snapshots and wrapped key data; it is available only after you export and permanently delete your Cloud Library, since existing items cannot be moved to a new key. Both preserve local data.
  • Delete account cancels the subscription, revokes sessions and devices, and deletes synced data and every Cloud Library item. If the cancellation fails, nothing is deleted and you can try again. Stripe and Link retain records required by law; the one-trial anti-abuse record described in Section 9 remains.
  • Local extension data stays in your browser until you remove it or uninstall the extension.

12 Security

Controls include HTTPS, local authenticated encryption for sync and Cloud Library data, encryption bound to each account and item, opaque random identifiers, hashed tokens and codes, short access-token lifetime, rotating refresh tokens, sign-in attempt limits, per-email, per-IP, and per-account rate limits, device approval, webhook HMAC verification, idempotent webhook processing, five-device enforcement, encrypted restore points, and deletion controls.

The encryption key and private device key are stored in browser local storage so the extension and web library can work without asking for the recovery code every time. Anyone who fully controls your browser profile may be able to access them. No electronic system is absolutely secure.
