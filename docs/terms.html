Plus adds a Cloud Library for chapters you save, a Release Agenda for the series you watch, encrypted sync of selected extension data with restore history, device management, and a web portal and web library. Eligible verified accounts receive one 30-day trial after completing a checkout that requires a payment method. Unless cancelled before the trial ends, the subscription renews automatically at US$1.50 per month plus taxes shown at checkout. We email a reminder seven days before the trial ends. Trial eligibility does not reset when an account is deleted and recreated.

It is then deleted, and we email a warning seven days before.

The Cloud Library is personal storage. Items are private to your account, and Plus has no sharing or publishing feature. Each account has storage and item limits shown in the Cloud Library. We may change the limits for future uploads with notice; lowering a limit never deletes items already stored. Items in the trash are permanently deleted after seven days.

The Cloud Library is not a backup service. Keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose; we do not guarantee that stored items will remain available.
