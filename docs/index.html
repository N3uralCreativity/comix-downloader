Per-chapter control

Every chapter, its own button.

The extension injects a download control into each row of the chapter list, exactly where you're already looking. Grab a single update or cherry-pick a back-catalogue.

  • Inline, no extra clicks or new tabs
  • Consistent naming & numbering
  • Works on every title page

Library-ready

Library-grade, and smart about it.

Save as CBZ with ComicInfo.xml and a cover — drop-in ready for Komga, Kavita, Mihon and YACReader. Pick everything from a simple panel when you hit Download All, or just press Start.

  • CBZ + metadata + Kavita/Komga folder layout
  • Skip already-downloaded — grab only what's new
  • Subscribe & get notified of new chapters
  • Optional push straight to your own media server
