Save it once. Read it anywhere.

Comix Downloader Plus is the optional subscription we are building alongside the free extension. It keeps the chapters you save in an encrypted Cloud Library you can read on any device, shows when the series you follow are likely to return, and syncs your setup across browsers.

01 · Cloud Library

Your comics, in a library that follows you.

Download All gets a new destination. Choose PLUS Cloud Library, or this device and the cloud together, and every chapter uploads into your library, already organized. Open it in any browser you have approved, on a computer or a phone, and pick up where you stopped.

  1. 1

    Choose the cloud

    In the Download All options, pick PLUS Cloud Library under Save to. Ranges, specific chapters and group filters work exactly as before.

  2. 2

    It files itself

    Each comic gets a folder with its chapters inside. Prefer your own structure? Save into any folder you created.

  3. 3

    Read anywhere

    Open the library in any browser you sign in to with your email. The reader scrolls vertically or turns pages, left to right or right to left.

  • Pick up where you stopped

    Your reading position is saved, so another device opens on the same page.

  • Read offline

    Keep chapters on a device for flights, commutes and patchy Wi-Fi.

  • Organize like a drive

    Folders in folders, rename, move, search, select several, trash and restore.

  • Bring your own files

    Import CBZ files or folders of images, and export any chapter back to CBZ.

  • Uploads that resume

    An interrupted upload waits in Transfers and continues where it stopped.

  • Installs like an app

    Add the library to your desktop or home screen on browsers that support it.

Encrypted before it leaves your browser.

Pages, titles, folder names, thumbnails and reading positions are encrypted on your device before upload. The service keeps a protected copy of your key so a code sent to your email is all you need on a new device, and it does not open your library. The storage included with Plus will be announced before launch.

02 · Release Agenda

Know which days your series are likely to return.

The Agenda reads the upload history of each series you follow and places its next chapter on the most likely day of the week. It opens as a page inside comix.to, in the site's own look, one click from the header, with the evidence behind every placement.

On schedule
A steady interval across recent chapters. The day follows from it.
Estimated
Worked out from fewer or looser samples, so the day can move.
Late
The expected day has passed and the chapter has not appeared yet.
Unscheduled
Too little or too irregular a history to place a useful date.

Every date is an estimate of availability on comix.to, never an official release date. Agenda history stays on your device and is not part of Sync.

03 · Sync

Your setup, on every browser you read on.

Choose exactly what travels between up to five browsers. Everything is encrypted on your device before it syncs, and thirty days of restore points undo a change you regret.

  • Settings, watched series, reading progress, download markers and stats, each one opt-in
  • Sign in on a new browser with a code sent to your email, and remove any browser from the Plus panel
  • AES-256-GCM on your device, with no recovery code to keep
  • Daily restore points for 30 days, and a safety point before every restore

Comic files never travel through Sync. That is what the Cloud Library is for.

One click from the toolbar.

Sign in from the extension popup with a one-time code sent to your email. Once you are in, its Plus row opens Cloud Library and the Release Agenda directly, with no trip through the settings.

Free stays free. Downloads, ZIP and CBZ, subscriptions and reader tools remain accountless and unchanged, before and after Plus ships.

Questions

Can I subscribe or sign in now?

Not yet. Plus is in private testing, so accounts are not open to the public.

How much cloud storage will it include?

That is being sized during testing and will be announced before launch.

Can the service read my comics?

Pages and names are encrypted on your device before upload. The service keeps a protected copy of your key so an email code can unlock a new device, which means it could technically open your data. It does not, except where the law requires.

Do I need the extension to read on my phone?

No. Saving from comix.to uses the extension, but reading happens in the Cloud Library in any browser where you sign in with your email.

Will the free extension change?

No. Nothing that works today moves behind an account.

Is the price final?

US$1.50/month with a 30-day trial is the current plan. It can still change before release.
