A preview of what Plus will be.

Plus is an optional subscription we are building alongside the free extension: continuity across the browsers you read from, reading migration between platforms, and external sources. Nothing here is live yet, and the free extension is not waiting on it.

Planned: 30 days freethen US$1.50/mo + tax

Pricing and trial length are the current plan, not a commitment. Nothing is charged today.

Want to know when it opens? Watch releases on GitHub — the launch is announced there and in the changelog.

Five approved devices

Settings, watched series and reading positions on every browser you approve.

Reading migration

Carry your progress between reading platforms, and read from external sources.

Thirty days of restore

Roll back a wrong change without losing the state you had before it.

Where it stands

  1. Now

    Building and testing

    Sync, devices and restore are running in internal builds. The panel above is one of them.

  2. Next

    Closed beta

    A small group tests migration and external sources on real libraries before anything is charged.

  3. Later

    Public release

    Sign-up opens with the trial. Announced in the changelog and on GitHub releases.

Free stays free. Downloads, ZIP, CBZ, subscriptions and reader tools remain accountless and unchanged, before and after Plus ships.

Questions

Can I subscribe or sign in now?

Not yet. Plus is in development, so there is no account to sign in to until release.

When does it launch?

No date is set. It ships when the beta holds up, and it is announced in the changelog.

Will the free extension change?

No. Nothing that works today moves behind an account.

Is the price final?

US$1.50/month with a 30-day trial is the current plan. It can still change before release.

Everything on this page describes work in progress. Features, pricing and timing may change before release.
