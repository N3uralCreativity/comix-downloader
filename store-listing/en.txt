SUMMARY
Save chapters from comix.to and comix.ws as ZIP or CBZ with Download All, progress tracking, recovery, and popup blocking.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
Comix Downloader is an unofficial, open-source browser extension for comix.to and comix.ws.

It adds download controls directly to title and chapter lists so you can save one chapter or a complete series as ZIP or CBZ.

Key features:
- Individual and full-series downloads
- Concurrent chapter fetching with live progress
- Separate download, ZIP creation, and save-confirmation stages
- Recovery checkpoints and clear handling of canceled saves
- Configurable filenames, chapter folders, format-specific archive splitting, and ComicInfo metadata
- Blocking for Comix's intrusive click ads, popunders, and transparent overlays, enabled by default and configurable in Settings
- Optional reading statistics, resume tools, subscriptions, and push-to-library support

Core downloads, settings, history, and reading statistics are processed locally in your browser. Built-in profile badges, community chapter flags, and extension notices contact the project's Cloudflare service using hashed identifiers or the extension version. Push-to-library sends files only to the server you configure. See the privacy policy for complete details.

Comix Downloader is not affiliated with comix.to or comix.ws. Only save content that you are authorized to download.
